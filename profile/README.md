## About

Fasthand Studio is a development team and a community of builders. We design and ship the FastHand marketplace, and we build the tools, libraries, and infrastructure that surround it.

Some of our work becomes a product. Some becomes a tool other developers can use. Some stays an experiment. All of it is made by people who enjoy the work.

---

## FastHand

FastHand is a fast marketplace built for Bangladesh.

The idea behind it is simple: buying and selling online should be quick, and the software should be a pleasure to use. We are building FastHand as a complete system, from the customer experience to seller tooling, payments, logistics, and identity verification, rather than as a storefront alone.

### Platform areas

| Area | Focus |
| :--- | :--- |
| Marketplace | Browsing, search, and deal discovery |
| Sellers | Tools for stores and businesses to list, manage, and sell |
| Verification | Identity and business verification |
| Logistics | Fulfillment and delivery across the marketplace |
| Payments | Checkout and commerce infrastructure |
| Interface | Carefully designed, consistent product UI |
